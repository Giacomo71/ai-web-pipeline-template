# Client Questionnaire

1. What is the project name?
2. What business problem should it solve?
3. Who are the target users?
4. What are the top user goals?
5. Which pages or screens are required?
6. Which features are essential for v1?
7. Which features are explicitly out of scope?
8. Is authentication required?
9. What user roles exist?
10. What data must be stored?
11. Which third-party integrations are required?
12. Are payments required?
13. Are transactional emails required?
14. Which analytics tools are needed?
15. What SEO requirements exist?
16. What privacy/compliance constraints apply?
17. What visual references or brand guidelines exist?
18. What accessibility requirements apply?
19. What performance expectations apply?
20. What is the launch deadline and budget constraint?
